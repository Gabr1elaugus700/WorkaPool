# Metas

Sales targets by representative, product group, and period.

## Language

**Meta**:
A sales target for a Representante over a period, usually scoped by product group.
_Avoid_: Goal (in speech — same concept; prefer Meta), Pedido, Carga

**Representante**:
The sales rep the Meta applies to (codRep). Same identity as in Pedidos.
_Avoid_: User (unless you mean the login account that owns or edits metas)
